

# Introduction to Computer Science for Mathematics and Physics Graduate Students and Researchers

This document is written for mathematics and physics graduate students/researchers who have never studied computer science (CS), such as programming. I believe that **it is beneficial for math/physics graduate students and researchers to acquire CS skills**. The reasons are:
1. Computer knowledge can directly impact research activities, such as numerical computation and LaTeX configuration,
2. You can use computers more efficiently and automate routine research-related tasks,

These are the two main points. Based on this, this repository was created out of my desire to help newcomers, and is written specifically for those with no prior knowledge of these technologies.
